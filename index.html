Innovation + Responsibility

Sustainability Through the Ages

Discover how Intel combines innovation and responsibility to build a more sustainable future for technology and our planet.

1968

Intel Founded

Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore rename NM Electronics as Intel Corporation, beginning a lasting legacy of technological innovation.

1971

First Microprocessor

Intel introduces the 4004, the world's first commercial microprocessor, helping launch a new era of smaller and more powerful computers.

1978

8086 Processor

Intel launches the 8086 processor, introducing the x86 architecture that would power generations of personal computers and servers.

1985

386 Processor

Intel introduces the 386 processor and its 32-bit architecture, bringing faster performance and improved multitasking to personal computers.

2006

Peak GHG Emissions

Intel reaches its highest annual operational greenhouse gas emissions in 2006. In the years that follow, the company invests in cleaner manufacturing, renewable energy, and emissions-reduction technologies.

2020

RISE Strategy

Intel introduces its RISE (Responsible, Inclusive, Sustainable, Enabling) strategy and 2030 goals to support climate action, protect water resources, and reduce waste across the industry.

2022

Net-Zero By 2040

Intel commits to reaching net-zero Scope 1 and 2 greenhouse gas emissions across its global operations by 2040, building on years of environmental progress.

2023

Renewable Electricity

Intel reaches 99% renewable electricity use worldwide, reducing carbon emissions and moving closer to its long-term sustainability goals.

2024

Sustainability Summit

Intel hosts its first Sustainability Summit, bringing suppliers, government officials, and industry leaders together to advance more sustainable semiconductor manufacturing.
